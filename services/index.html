Technical SEO Audit

Make sure your website isn't working against you.

Your content can be great, but if search engines struggle to access or understand your website, it can limit how well your pages perform.

I look for technical problems that could be affecting your visibility, including issues that aren't always obvious when browsing the site.

What I'll look at

  • Crawling and indexing: Can Google find and access your important pages?
  • Page speed and mobile performance: How quickly does your website load, and what could be slowing it down?
  • Schema markup: Is your structured data implemented correctly?
  • Redirects and duplicate content: Are technical issues creating unnecessary problems for search engines?
  • Website structure and internal links: Can visitors and search engines navigate your content easily?
  • CMS and migration issues: Are there problems with URLs, redirects or website changes that could affect SEO?

What you'll get

A clear breakdown of the issues found, their potential impact and practical recommendations for fixing them, organised by priority.

Content Strategy Audit

Find the keywords, content gaps and opportunities you're missing.

Publishing more content isn't always the answer. Sometimes the biggest opportunity is improving pages you already have.

I review your existing content, research what your potential customers are searching for and compare your website with competitors to identify where you could perform better.

What I'll look at

  • Keyword research and mapping: Which searches are relevant to your business, and which pages should target them?
  • Search intent: Does your content answer what people are actually looking for?
  • Competitor content gaps: Which valuable topics are your competitors covering that you're missing?
  • Internal linking and topic structure: Are your pages connected in a way that helps users and search engines?
  • Content optimisation: Which pages should be refreshed, expanded, combined or removed?
  • Content planning: What should you prioritise next, and what should new content cover?

What you'll get

A practical content improvement plan, keyword opportunities and prioritised recommendations for your existing and future pages.

AI Visibility Audit

Find out how your business appears in AI search.

People increasingly use tools such as ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity to research services, compare businesses and find recommendations.

But does your business actually appear in those answers? And if it doesn't, who gets mentioned instead?

I investigate how your business is represented in AI-generated answers and look for opportunities to make your content and business information clearer and more useful.

What I'll look at

  • Current AI visibility: Does your business appear for relevant questions?
  • Competitor visibility: Which businesses are mentioned or cited instead?
  • Accuracy of information: Do AI tools describe your services and business correctly?
  • Content and website structure: Could clearer information help AI tools understand your business?
  • Brand mentions and credibility: How is your business represented across relevant websites and public sources?
  • AI referral traffic: Where measurable, are AI tools bringing visitors to your website?

What you'll get

A snapshot of your current AI visibility, competitor comparisons and recommendations for improving how your business is represented online.

AI-generated answers change regularly, so this is an assessment of current visibility, not a guarantee of future mentions.

Trust & Reputation Audit

Give people a reason to trust what they find.

Getting visitors to your website is one thing. Making them feel confident enough to contact you, book a service or make a purchase is another.

I look at the information and trust signals people encounter when researching your business, both on your website and elsewhere online.

What I'll look at

  • About pages and author information: Is it clear who's behind the business or content?
  • Contact details and transparency: Can customers easily find important business information?
  • Reviews and reputation: What do customer reviews and public feedback say about your business?
  • Content credibility: Are important claims supported by experience, evidence or reliable sources?
  • External mentions: How consistently is your business represented across relevant websites?
  • Trust and user experience: Are there obvious issues that might make visitors hesitant to take the next step?

What you'll get

An assessment of your website's trust signals and online reputation, with specific recommendations to strengthen credibility and improve the information potential customers see.

What happens after an audit?

You won't receive a long report full of problems with no explanation of what to do about them.

Every audit includes

  • A clear summary of the main findings.
  • Examples showing what needs attention.
  • A prioritised list of recommended improvements.
  • Practical guidance on how to make those changes.
  • Recommendations you can act on yourself or share with your developer or content team.

Some checks benefit from access to Google Search Console or Google Analytics. We'll agree on what's needed before starting.
