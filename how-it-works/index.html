How it works

Clear advice, practical recommendations and a process that makes sense.

You don't need to know exactly what's wrong with your website before reaching out. We'll start with what you're trying to achieve, look at where things stand and work out what needs attention.

  1. Free intro call

    Let's start with a conversation.

    Tell me a little about your business, share your website and let me know what you'd like to improve.

    I'll take a quick look at your site before we speak, so we can make the most of our conversation.

    During a free 20–30 minute call, we'll discuss your goals, any challenges you've noticed and whether an audit or a more specific SEO service would be the best fit.

    There's no obligation to go ahead.

  2. Website audit

    Time to look at what's actually happening.

    Once we've agreed on the scope and price, I'll review the relevant areas of your website, look at available search data and compare your site with competitors where appropriate.

    Depending on the service, this might involve technical SEO, keyword research, content performance, AI visibility or trust signals.

    I'll explain what access I need beforehand. Some checks can be completed using publicly available information, while others benefit from Google Search Console or Analytics access.

  3. Prioritised action plan

    Know what needs fixing and where to start.

    You'll receive a report explaining what I've found, why it matters and what I'd recommend doing about it.

    I organise recommendations by their potential impact and the effort involved, so you can focus on what matters most rather than trying to fix everything at once.

    The goal is to give you a plan you can actually use, whether you're managing the website yourself or have a team helping you.

  4. Walkthrough and next steps

    Let's go through the findings together.

    I'll walk you through the recommendations, explain anything that's unclear and answer your questions.

    From there, you can implement the changes yourself, share the plan with your team or ask me to help with specific improvements or ongoing SEO support.

    There's no requirement to commit to additional services.
